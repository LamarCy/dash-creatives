# The Keeper — creature notes

**Origin.** Straight out of *Lowcountry Beach*: "I bet it's dolphins who know /
where the ocean gon' hide / the treasures of my soul." The creature is the one
who knows where the treasures are — so it guards, it doesn't fight. No franchise
lineage: a dolphin drawn from a spine curve, four values, original silhouette.

**Working name.** The Keeper.

**Alternates for Durrell to pick from:**

1. **The Keeper** *(current)* — plainest and strongest. "Keeper" carries both
   custodian and the fisherman's sense of a fish worth keeping, which is very
   Lowcountry. Reads well in Oswald caps: `THE KEEPER`.
2. **Treasure** — pulled directly from the lyric. Warmer, more affectionate,
   works if the creature becomes merch or a mascot for kids. Slight risk: it
   describes the cargo rather than the character.
3. **Sable** — a name rather than a title, so the dolphin becomes a specific
   individual. Sable Island / sable = dark; pairs with the ink-heavy night
   variant. Gives you room for a second creature later without renaming.
4. **Pilot** — dolphins pilot boats in the harbor; a pilot guides you home.
   Nautical, Charleston-specific, and short enough for a cartridge label.

My pick if you want one: **the Keeper** for the world, with **Treasure** held in
reserve as the affectionate nickname in captions. Say which and I'll rename the
files and folders.
